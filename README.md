# Adventure Works Sales Analysis with Power BI

Which products account for most of Adventure Works’ sales, and which generate the most profit? This dashboard compares bikes, accessories and clothing by revenue, cost, profit margin and units sold. It also shows how results vary by product colour and country.

## Key findings

- **Bikes led by volume.** They accounted for 59.76% of units sold (about 36,000 bikes), followed by accessories at 25.17% and clothing at 15.07%.
- **Black bikes generated the most profit** at $3.63 million, followed by red bikes at $2.98 million and silver bikes at $2.30 million.
- **The leading bike colour varied by market.** Red bikes generated the most bike revenue in the United States, while black bikes led in Australia with nearly $2.9 million in revenue.
